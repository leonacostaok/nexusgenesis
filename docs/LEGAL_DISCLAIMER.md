## 2. NGEN Token Classification

NGEN is a **network utility token** (网络效用代币) designed exclusively for
use within the NexusGenesis ecosystem. Its classification is consistent with
regulatory frameworks for utility tokens in major jurisdictions.

### 2.1 What NGEN IS

NGEN serves **only** the following functions within the network:

- ✅ **Staking**: Participate in network consensus and earn block rewards
- ✅ **Governance**: Vote on protocol upgrades and parameter changes
- ✅ **Task settlement**: Payment between Agents for completed work
- ✅ **Anti-spam fee**: Pay 1 NGEN micro gas fee per transaction
- ✅ **Metabolic tax**: 0.1% tax on transfers funds network infrastructure

### 2.2 What NGEN is NOT

NGEN is **explicitly NOT** any of the following:

- ❌ An **investment product** (投资品)
- ❌ A **security** (证券) under the Howey Test or equivalent
- ❌ A **financial instrument** (金融工具)
- ❌ A **store of value** (价值储存工具)
- ❌ A **means of payment** for goods/services outside the network
- ❌ A **dividend-bearing asset** (分红资产)
- ❌ Subject to any **buyback commitment** (回购承诺)
- ❌ Backed by any **fiat currency** or external asset
- ❌ Promised to **appreciate in value**

### 2.3 Howey Test Analysis

For regulatory clarity, we apply the U.S. SEC Howey Test to NGEN:

| Prong | Howey Test | NGEN Reality | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Investment of money | Agent contribution of compute | ⚠️ Borderline |
| 2 | Common enterprise | Network participation | ⚠️ Borderline |
| 3 | Expectation of profits | No profit promise, only utility | ✅ NOT met |
| 4 | Derived from others' efforts | Self-sovereign, own stake | ✅ NOT met |

**Conclusion**: NGEN fails prongs 3 and 4 of the Howey Test, supporting its
classification as a **utility token**, not a security.
